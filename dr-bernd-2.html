Before we begin

Doctor Bernd will present a pediatric patient and question you on the differential diagnosis, the tests you would recommend and the treatment. His questions adapt to your answers.

  • Ask for the history, examination and test results you need. Nothing is handed to you.
  • Press Hold to pause the conversation at any time, and Continuation to pick up where you left off. A held session is kept in this browser, even if you close the page.
  • Type stop (on its own) or press Stop and review to end. You will get a question-by-question review, your evaluation and recommended reading.
