Before we begin

Dr. Bernd will present a pediatric patient and question you, adapting to your answers. Talk with the microphone button or type.

  • Ask for the history, exam and test results you need. Nothing is handed to you.
  • Say or type stop at any time to end. You'll get a question-by-question review, your evaluation and reading recommendations.
  • Voice input works best in Chrome, Edge or Safari. Allow microphone access when asked.
